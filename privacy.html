Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who uses this app

Elvis Home Assistant is a personal tool used only by its developer, Elvis Bistrovs, with his own Google accounts. It isn't published to or used by anyone else.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail: reads message headers and content to summarise new mail. It may apply labels to the user's own messages.
  • Google Calendar: reads and creates events in the user's own calendars.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the user's own computer to produce a daily summary saved in his private notes.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on that computer only.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
